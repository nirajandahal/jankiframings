Traditional Mandir Vastu & Placement Guidelines

For Hindu Gods' Pooja frames (Ram Darbar, Radha Krishna, Balaji, Shiv Parivar), Vastu Shastra recommends hanging on the East or North-East wall of your home or pooja room. Maintain a height where the deity's feet are positioned at eye level or above during worship.

Gold Foil & Glass Cleaning Tips:

  • The gold foil artwork is sealed behind airtight glass; clean the outer sheet glass gently using a soft dry microfiber cloth.
  • Avoid spraying alcohol or harsh chemical glass cleaners directly onto the frame joints.
  • Do not allow oil droplets from brass diyas or incense ash to accumulate directly on natural teakwood borders.
